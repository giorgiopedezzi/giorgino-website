## Resulting flow

1. **Worldview:** Hero, beliefs, and Dark Matter form one opening argument about judgment, systems, and responsibility.
2. **Where it goes:** Thinking points to the editorial work instead of reproducing it.
3. **Proof through making:** Running / Building shows a constrained AI-assisted experiment, not a product pitch.
4. **Personal turn:** Human opens the route to the fuller personal narrative.
5. **Continuation:** Contact closes the page.

The visual sequence remains intentionally familiar; the structural reduction is semantic. The long personal arc and full personal narrative are removed from the homepage content record and become the authoritative content of the Really About Me page.
