# US06 — Really About Me Deep Page

Build `Really About Me` as a reward for someone who kept reading, not as a conventional About page.

## Tone
Intimate, playful, ironic, spacious, fragmentary, elegant.

Notebook feeling comes from editing and placement, not decoration.

Do NOT add fake tape, fake Polaroids, fake handwriting, scrapbook clichés or decorative nostalgia.

## Preserve fragments
- Still here?
- Thank you. From the heart.
- real human / not a plausible one / Real.
- Every ten years, apparently, I need a new bad idea
- Missing Man
- Mortadella pizza
- WAIT WAIT WAIT
- The nonnino
- The book
- The dreams hunter runs free.

Do not explain lessons that can be inferred.

## Home relationship
The homepage should give the full picture while preserving curiosity and flow.
Really About Me should be:
- discoverable late in the homepage
- easy for returning visitors
- not promoted as an early shortcut CTA

## Assets
Use real artifacts when available:
- Missing Man GIF
- WAIT WAIT WAIT T-shirt photo
- other real images

If missing, keep honest placeholders rather than inventing assets.

## Final response
Report:
1. structure implemented
2. homepage handoff behavior
3. files changed
4. missing artifacts/content

Do not start another US.
